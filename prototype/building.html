The building and the selected floor

Select a floor in the building. Its office opens on the right, with one desk and one pixel-art character for each employee. Add employees to see the office adapt.

    • Room: one generated image of an empty room, the same for all floors.
    • Desk: one generated image. The page puts one desk on the floor for each employee.
    • Layout: the page computes a grid on the floor. More employees give more and smaller desks, up to 20 in this prototype.
    • Agents and name tags: each agent stands behind their desk and faces you. The page draws the tag with the name and the status.
